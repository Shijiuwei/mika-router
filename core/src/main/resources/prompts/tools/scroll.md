Scroll to see more of a page or panel. direction=down shows what is further down (like reading on; the finger swipes up), up shows what is further up (back toward the top), right shows what is further right, left what is further left. x/y optionally pick the panel to scroll (normalized 0-999, give both). effect.kind no_effect (with atEnd) means nothing moved in that direction.
