Disclaimer, data and privacy

简体中文 | English

Beta notes

  • This is an early public beta. Features and UI are still changing quickly, and upgrades may include breaking changes.
  • So far it has mainly been tested on one Android 14 phone. Other Android versions and other phones may hit problems we have not seen yet; reports are welcome.
  • Mika operates third-party apps and websites on your behalf. Please read the disclaimer below first.

Disclaimer

  • Operating third-party apps and websites through automation may violate their terms of service and carries a risk of restricted features or account bans. Use your own judgment, at your own risk.
  • You are responsible for actions you approve (and for actions Mika takes on your behalf in Autonomous mode). For anything important, use Ask mode and check the results (see Ask and Autonomous).
  • This is beta software, provided "as is", without warranty of any kind (see LICENSE).

Data and privacy

  • There is no Mika server. What you say, screen contents and screenshots go only to the model provider you configure.
  • API keys, memories and your user profile are encrypted with the Android Keystore and stored on the phone. Conversations, run records and token usage stay on the device.
  • Update checks only contact GitHub.
  • The built-in browser visits only the sites you send it to; the sandbox is online by default, and you can turn it off in Settings.

License and third-party components

Mika is released under the Apache License 2.0; see NOTICE for the copyright notice.

Third-party components keep their own licenses; see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. In particular, the APK contains standalone executables libproot.so and libproot_loader.so built from PRoot (GPL-2.0-or-later, statically linked with talloc, LGPL-3.0-or-later); Mika only runs them as child processes. Source locations and checksums are in tools/sandbox/sources.lock, patches are in tools/sandbox/patches/, and they can be rebuilt with tools/sandbox/build-native.sh. The sandbox runtime (a minimal Alpine Linux 3.22 root filesystem and packages) ships with the APK, and the matching source packages are attached to each release; see tools/sandbox/README.md for how.
